The Paul 50 Bag — plugin (test build)

Mac
1. Unzip.
2. Copy "The Paul 50 Bag.component" to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   Copy "The Paul 50 Bag.vst3"      to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
3. These test builds are not notarised yet. Once, in Terminal:
     xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"The Paul 50 Bag.component" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"The Paul 50 Bag.vst3"
4. Restart your DAW and look for "SamSonight · The Paul 50 Bag" under instruments.
"The Paul 50 Bag.app" is a standalone version (no DAW needed).

In a DAW the band follows the host tempo and plays while the transport runs.
A MIDI note sets the key of the whole band.
